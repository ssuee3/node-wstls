One Planet, One Future

Preserving our vast oceans and safeguarding our precious land.

1. Protect Our Oceans

The ocean is not a trash can. Every year, millions of tons of plastic enter our waters, threatening marine life. Moreover, illegal wastewater discharge is silently poisoning the source of life on Earth.

  • Stop Littering: Always dispose of trash properly and reduce your use of single-use plastics.
  • Zero Toxic Discharge: Advocate against the illegal dumping of industrial and chemical wastewater into the sea.
  • Marine Biodiversity: Clean water is essential for the survival of coral reefs and endangered sea creatures.

2. Safeguard Our Land

Land is the foundation of human survival. Soil pollution is often invisible and long-lasting; once damaged, it can take centuries to recover.

  • Prevent Soil Degradation: Use land responsibly and strictly prohibit the dumping of industrial waste on agricultural soil.
  • Smart Agriculture: Reduce the excessive use of pesticides and chemical fertilizers to prevent heavy metal accumulation.
  • Reforestation: Plant trees to prevent soil erosion and desertification, ensuring a green legacy for future generations.
